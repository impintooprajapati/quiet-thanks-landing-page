Privacy Policy for Quiet Thanks

Effective Date: July 19, 2026

Thank you for using Quiet Thanks.

Your privacy is the foundation of this app. Quiet Thanks is designed to work entirely on your device. We do not operate servers that store your journal, we do not require an account, and we do not collect personal information.

100% Local Storage

Your journal lives strictly on your phone with local encryption.

No Account Needed

Zero signups, emails, passwords, phone numbers, or logins.

Zero Tracking or Ads

No telemetry, analytics, third-party trackers, or advertisements.

Full Data Ownership

Export, back up, or permanently wipe your data at any time.

01

Information We Collect

Quiet Thanks does not collect, transmit, or store your personal information on our servers.

Everything you create remains on your device unless you choose to export or back it up yourself.

Your journal entries may include:

  • ✓ Journal text — The thoughts, moments, and words you write.
  • ✓ Photos you attach — Visual memories stored within your app directory.
  • ✓ Mood selections — Emotion ratings paired with your entries.
  • ✓ Streak information — Your consecutive days and activity counters.
  • ✓ App preferences — Settings such as theme and reminder time.
  • ✓ Passcode settings — Stored securely and encrypted on your device.

This information never leaves your device automatically.

02

How Your Data Is Stored

Your journal is stored locally on your device.

Quiet Thanks uses encrypted local storage to help protect your journal from unauthorized access.

Your information is not uploaded to our servers because Quiet Thanks does not use cloud storage.

03

Backups

Quiet Thanks allows you to manually export your journal.

If you choose to create a backup, you decide where it is stored, such as:

  • ✓Files app
  • ✓iCloud Drive
  • ✓Google Drive
  • ✓Another storage location you select

These backups are created only when you request them.

We do not receive, access, or store your backup files.

04

Permissions

Depending on the features you use, Quiet Thanks may request permission to access:

  • ✓ Photos: Used only when you choose to attach an image to an entry.
  • ✓ Camera (Optional): Used only if you choose to take a photo directly from the app.
  • ✓ Microphone (Optional): Used for on-device voice-to-text when creating journal entries. Audio is processed on your device whenever supported by your operating system.
  • ✓ Notifications (Optional): Used to send optional daily reminders. Notification scheduling happens locally on your device.
05

No Account Required

Quiet Thanks does not require:

  • ✕Sign up
  • ✕Login
  • ✕Email address
  • ✕Phone number
  • ✕Social media account

You can begin using the app immediately without providing any personal credentials.

06

No Advertising

Quiet Thanks contains no third-party advertising.

We do not display personalized advertisements.

We do not share your information with advertising networks.

07

No Analytics or Tracking

Quiet Thanks does not use analytics services that send your information to external servers.

We do not track:

  • ✕Your activity
  • ✕Your journal content
  • ✕Your usage behavior
  • ✕Your location

Your journal remains strictly private.

08

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information.

Since we do not collect your journal data, there is nothing for us to share.

09

Data Deletion

You are in complete control of your information. You can delete:

  • ✓Individual journal entries
  • ✓Photos attached to entries
  • ✓Backup files you created
  • ✓All app data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app

Deleting the app removes locally stored data unless you have created your own backup.

10

Children's Privacy

Quiet Thanks is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because the app does not collect user data, there is no personal information transmitted to us.

11

Security

We use reasonable security measures including encrypted local storage to help protect your journal.

However, no method of storage can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and any backup files you create.

12

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published with a revised Effective Date.
